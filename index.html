Privacy Policy

How Kisan Smart Hisab handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: 3 November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Kisan Smart Hisab (“the App”), a farming and daily accounting calculator application. It explains the information that may be handled while you use the App.

1. Information You Enter

The App is intended to let users perform calculations and maintain records such as farming expenses, land measurements, labour charges, weights, customer accounts, and other everyday calculations. Information you enter into these features is intended to be stored locally on your device.

Please avoid entering passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information into calculation or notes fields.

2. Account and Login

The version covered by this policy is described as not requiring an account or login to use its features. If a future version introduces login, cloud backup, or account-related functionality, this policy should be updated before that version is released.

3. Local Storage

Records saved by the App are intended to remain on your device rather than being sent to a developer-operated cloud database. Data stored locally may be lost if you uninstall the App, clear its storage, reset your device, or lose access to the device, unless you have made a separate backup.

4. Device Permissions and Diagnostics

The App may request device permissions only when needed for a feature. The actual permissions and any diagnostic or crash-reporting services depend on the version installed. This policy does not claim that analytics or crash reporting is enabled. Check the published app to confirm which permissions and third-party SDKs it actually uses.

5. Third-Party Services

If a version of the App includes third-party services such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or external links, those providers may process information under their own privacy policies. Any third-party service actually included in the published version should be identified here with an appropriate link.

6. Sharing and Selling Information

We do not sell personal information. The App is designed for local calculations and records. If the app's implementation changes to transmit information to another party, this policy must be updated to describe that sharing accurately.

7. Data Security and Retention

Because records are intended to be stored locally, you are responsible for protecting access to your device and maintaining any backups you need. No electronic storage method is completely secure. Data may be removed when you delete it in the App or remove the App from your device, subject to the device's storage behavior.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose calculator and record-keeping tool and is not specifically designed to collect children's personal information. If you believe personal information has been collected improperly, contact the developer using the contact details published with the App.

9. Your Choices

You can choose what information to enter into the App and may delete records using any deletion controls provided by the App. Uninstalling the App may remove locally stored records. Back up any records you need before uninstalling or clearing app data.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The latest version should be published at the Privacy Policy URL listed on the app's distribution page.
